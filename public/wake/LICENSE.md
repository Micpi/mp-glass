# Wake word models

`melspectrogram.onnx`, `embedding_model.onnx` and `hey_jarvis.onnx` come from openWakeWord v0.5.1
(https://github.com/dscripka/openWakeWord, release assets). The code of openWakeWord is under the Apache 2.0 license;
its pre-trained models are under CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 (non-commercial use). The embedding model derives from Google's
`speech_embedding` (https://tfhub.dev/google/speech_embedding/1, Apache 2.0).

A model trained for your own home (for example « Nexus », with Home Assistant's training notebook) is yours and is
kept by Home Assistant, not in this folder.
